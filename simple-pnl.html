What happens if you put up a booth?

One equation drives the public-event business. A second drives pre-sold events. Change the yellow inputs; every dollar updates.

Base-case answer: start with pre-sold events, not a public booth.
Public-event base cases below lose money. A 75-person pre-sold event produces about $311 after modeled operating costs.
PUBLIC: min(foot traffic × penetration, booth capacity) × contribution per ticket − fixed event cost
PRE-SOLD: guaranteed guests × price − food − card fees − labor − overhead
