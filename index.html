Condiment and flavor lab

A strong dog needs contrast, not a pile. Operational rule: one sauce, one acidic component, one crunch or fresh finish; no more than three finishing motions.

Premium dog service specification

Frank4 oz, natural-casing, smoked all-beef frank. Start with a proven Bay Area producer; commission a custom grind only after repeat demand.
Warm155–160°F water bath; do not boil. Use plain or lightly seasoned water—the casing limits flavor transfer.
Finish45–75 seconds on a hot plancha, rolling for blister and color; verify required reheating temperature and serve immediately.
Bun70–85 g split-top milk/potato roll. Brush inside lightly and toast cut-side down 20–30 seconds.
Build15–18 g sauce + 20–25 g well-drained acid/slaw + 5–7 g dry crunch. Crunch always goes on last.
SpeedTarget 25 seconds finishing time and 30 completed tickets/hour per two-person line.

Lead concept: Bay Miso Crunch

Build
Natural-casing smoked beef frank
Toasted milk-potato roll
White-miso mustard — 16 g
Rice-vinegar cabbage — 22 g, squeezed dry
Sourdough-nori crunch — 6 g, added last

Why it is ownable: Japanese umami architecture plus a San Francisco sourdough crunch. It avoids the Kewpie-kimchi and terimayo lanes already occupied locally.

Crunch batch: toasted sourdough crumb + crispy shallot + nori + sesame. Hold dry; never mix it into wet slaw.

CULINARY RECOMMENDATION: confidence 7/10 pending blind taste and 30-minute hold tests. Natural-casing poach-and-grill technique: Serious Eats. Food safety: USDA FSIS. Local product overlap: Hayz Dog and JAPADOG.
